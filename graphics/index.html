Culture Icon Design

Icon design is an exercise in recognizable simplicity. An effective icon will add helpful context to headers, buttons, and forms. Less is always more with an icon so it’s important to start small and stop when your icon "screams" your keyword (music, shopping, messages, etc).

These music, news, and culture icons start from an isometric base and use black solids to create depth and volume. I paired these icons with overlapping colors to add a little organic contrast.

Event Branding

Event branding is something I’ve routinely done for LA’s #1 public radio station, KCRW. Renowned for its music and news programming, KCRW also brings all-star (and often free) events to Los Angeles year-round. KCRW’s Good Food Pie Contest is no exception featuring an afternoon of pie tasting, baking demos, kids activities and more.

The branding I created for this event features a "Mardi Gras" influenced logo design inspired by a special "New Orleans" pie category. In this design you can see subtle pie decoration patterns in the background, familiar carnival colors, and energetic "jazzy" letters. It’s important to start projects like these with the expectation that your design will be utilized many ways (signage, screen-prints, identification, and more).
